Waking the birds…

How to play

Your birds gather seeds every second, even while you're away (up to 12 hours). Spend them on more birds, garden pieces and treats.

Where you put things is everything.

  • Sparrows love company. Robins want room.
  • Chickadees boost every other species around them.
  • Cardinals pair up. Goldfinches crave sunflowers.
  • Bohemian waxwings chain into great flocks and feast on fruiting rowan trees.

Feeders, sunflowers and birdbaths boost birds near them. Pick something in the shop, then hover the grove: highlighted tiles show its reach, and the label shows the gain.

Golden Feathers are rare. They hatch from Nest Boxes kept warm by the birds around them. Spend them in the Golden shop on a bigger grove and powerful upgrades.

Drag pieces to rearrange them for free. Keyboard: arrow keys move the cursor, Enter places, selects or moves, Esc cancels. Your grove saves automatically.
